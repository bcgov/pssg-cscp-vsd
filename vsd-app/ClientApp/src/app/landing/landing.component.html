Crime Victim Assistance Program

Apply for benefits under the Crime Victim Assistance Act

The Crime Victim Assistance Program (CVAP) provides benefits to victims of violent crime, their immediate family members, and certain witnesses in accordance with the Crime Victim Assistance Act and its regulations.

To begin your application, choose how you would like to proceed. Signing in with your BC Services Card allows your information to be pre-filled and your application to be saved. You may also complete the application without signing in.

Sign In with BC Services Card

Use your BC Services Card to securely verify your identity. Signing in allows your personal information to be pre-filled, reducing the time needed to complete your application.

  • Identity verified automatically
  • Personal information pre-filled
  • Faster and more accurate submission

Don't have a BC Services Card? Learn how to get one

Continue Without Signing In

You can complete and submit your application without a BC Services Card. You will need to enter all your personal information manually.

  • No account required
  • Application submitted anonymously
  • All information entered manually

Your privacy is protected. Read our privacy policy

Need help? A local victim service program can assist you in completing this application. To locate a program near you, call VictimLink BC toll free at 1-800-563-0808.
